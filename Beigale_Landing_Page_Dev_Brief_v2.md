# Beigale Insights — Landing Page Brief

> **For Web Developer:** Design & Implementation Proposal  
> **Primary Goal:** Credibility & Vetting (Not Lead Gen / SEO)

---

## Executive Overview & Design System

### Purpose & Audience
This page exists for **credibility, not lead generation**. The primary audience consists of senior stakeholders vetting Beigale — bankers, M&A lawyers, or corporate development leads doing background checks prior to an initial call. 

The page must communicate **focus, seniority, and trustworthiness in under 60 seconds**. It is not built to rank on search engines, capture inbound leads, or convert cold traffic.

### Design System (Locked)

| Element | Specification |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Aesthetic** | Clean, modern, editorial. Minimalist layout with generous whitespace. No decoration for its own sake. |
| **Color Palette** | • **Base:** Off-white (`#F9F8F5`)<br>• **Accent (Sparse Use):** Teal (`#1D9E75`)<br>• **Dark Sections:** Deep Charcoal (`#2C2C2A`) |
| **Typography & Tone** | Plainspoken, confident, and direct. Short lines and concise paragraphs. Avoid dense text blocks entirely. |

### 6. Why Choose Us & Leadership

#### Core Differentiators (3×2 Grid Layout)

1. **Commercial Operators**  
   Senior executives who have built businesses, led commercial organizations, and made growth decisions — not career analysts.
2. **Independent Judgment**  
   We test the commercial case objectively. Buy-side only, with zero success fees tied to deal completion.
3. **Partner-Led Throughout**  
   Both founders lead every interview, analysis, and recommendation. Nothing is delegated to junior teams.
4. **Built for Mid-Market Deals**  
   Senior-level commercial diligence, scoped and paced for transactions where speed, focus, and direct partner involvement matter.
5. **Decision-Oriented**  
   Clear commercial conclusions, practical implications, and a distinct point of view — not just raw observations.
