# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Corporate acquirers and corp-dev teams, PE firms and portfolio companies, VC firms, and M&A lawyers/bankers/transaction advisors acting as referral sources. They land on this page already pre-vetting Beigale — before a first call or before making a referral into a live deal. They are reading fast, mid-diligence, and judging competence from tone and structure, not from volume of text.

## Product Purpose

Beigale Insights is a boutique M&A commercial due diligence firm. This page is a credibility-only one-pager, not a lead-generation funnel — it is not optimized for SEO, conversion volume, or cold traffic. Success is the page reading as focused, senior, and trustworthy in under a minute.

## Brand Personality

Plainspoken and trustworthy. Not clever, not witty. Senior and operator-led — the firm is run by executives who've carried P&L responsibility, not junior analytical teams, and the page's voice should reflect that directness.

## Anti-references

- Dense text blocks or long paragraphs anywhere on the page
- Decorative or filled icon sets (line icons only, one per point)
- Lead-capture forms of any kind (contact is email + WhatsApp link only)
- Client-logo strips or testimonials sections
- SaaS clichés: hero-metric template, identical card grids, gradient text, glassmorphism
- Large fills of the teal or gold accent colors — sparse use only
- Decorative motion or animation beyond subtle scroll-reveal

## Design Principles

1. Credibility over conversion — no lead-gen mechanics, no growth-hacking patterns, no urgency tactics.
2. Sparse accent discipline — teal (light sections) and gold (dark sections only) are used for small emphasis, never large fills, never mixed across background types.
3. Scannable structure over prose — short lines, bullet/tag/tile format, no dense paragraphs.
4. Dark background signals identity shift — reserved narrowly for Hero and About only (the "what we do" → "who we are" transition), not used decoratively elsewhere.
5. Operator-led tone — plainspoken and direct, never reading as marketing spin or growth-team copy.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

English only, LTR only — no second language or language switcher, ever. No other specific accessibility requirements stated; default to WCAG AA.
