Design the hero section for "Beigale Insights," a boutique commercial due diligence firm for mid-market M&A ($20–100M deals). The audience is bankers, lawyers, PE/VC firms and corp-dev leads pre-vetting the firm before a first call — the page must read as focused, senior, and trustworthy in under a minute. This is a credibility page, not a lead-gen page: no forms, no hype, no clutter.

**Tone:** Plainspoken and trustworthy. Not clever, not witty. Confident through restraint — clean and modern, no decoration for its own sake. Short lines, generous whitespace, nothing dense.

**Copy (use exactly, do not rewrite):**
- H1: "Independent commercial due diligence for mid-market M&A"
- Subheading: "We independently assess the commercial engine behind the deal. Financial and legal diligence confirm the deal is clean. We confirm it's worth it."
- Nav CTA button: "Get in Touch"

**Critical copy treatment:** In the subheading, "financial and legal" must visually contrast against "commercial" — e.g. one muted/lighter weight, the other in the accent color or bolder weight. This contrast is the core positioning claim of the whole page (financial/legal diligence is table stakes; commercial diligence is the gap Beigale fills). Don't lose this distinction in the layout.

**Colors:**
- Background: off-white `#F9F8F5`
- Accent (sparse only — never large fills, never a background block): teal `#1D9E75`
- Ink/text: near-black, derived from `#2C2C2A`

**Typography:**
- Headline: Newsreader (serif) — editorial, senior, not decorative
- Body/UI: Inter (sans) — clean, neutral
- If a single-family system reads cleaner, Inter alone (weight-differentiated) is an acceptable alternative

**Layout requirements:**
- Nav bar: logo/wordmark "Beigale Insights" left, anchor links (The Gap · Solution · Engagements · Who We Serve · About · Contact) center or right, "Get in Touch" button right
- Hero: H1 + subheading, generous vertical whitespace, no stock photography, no illustration — typography and whitespace carry the section
- No icons, no decorative graphics, no gradients, no drop shadows beyond minimal/subtle
- This section must not feel like a typical SaaS landing page (no big centered CTA button in the hero itself, no product screenshot, no "trusted by" logo strip) — restraint is the design language

**Deliverables — two frames:**
1. Desktop, 1440×900
2. Mobile, 390×844 (iPhone 12/13/14 standard width)

Show nav + hero only. Do not design beyond the hero section.
